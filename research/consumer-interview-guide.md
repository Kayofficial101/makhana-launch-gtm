# Consumer interview guide

## Who to speak with

Recruit 20 people in each group:

1. bought makhana at least twice in the last month
2. bought it before but not in the last three months
3. buys salty snacks weekly but rarely chooses makhana

Keep Delhi NCR residence and online grocery use as screening criteria. Record age and occupation for context, but do not recruit only friends or only students.

## Interview rules

- Ask about the last real purchase before showing a concept.
- Do not describe the product as healthy before asking what matters.
- Avoid “Would you buy this?” until the end.
- Ask for trade-offs: what would this replace and what would make it lose?
- Record exact words and separate them from the interviewer's interpretation.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last salty snack you bought. Where were you and what was happening?
2. How did you choose it?
3. What other options did you consider?
4. When did you last buy makhana? Which brand, flavour, pack and price do you remember?
5. What did you like or dislike after opening the pack?
6. Has a makhana pack ever disappointed you? What went wrong?
7. When would you choose chips instead? When would you choose nuts instead?
8. Where do you normally buy snacks: quick commerce, supermarket, office canteen, marketplace or elsewhere?
9. How many people share the pack?
10. Which matters most: crunch, flavour, portion, ingredients, calories, protein, price or brand? Ask for the top two.
11. What would make you distrust a pack or its claims?
12. Show the unpriced concept. What do you think it is for? What feels unclear?
13. Ask one price per person: ₹129, ₹149 or ₹169 for 50 g. Would you definitely buy, probably buy, might buy, probably not or definitely not?
14. What would you buy instead at that price?
15. What would have to be true for you to buy it again next week?

## Output

For every interview, capture the last purchase, occasion, alternative considered, failure story, channel, top two decision factors, tested price and an exact quote. Summarise patterns only after all interviews are complete.
