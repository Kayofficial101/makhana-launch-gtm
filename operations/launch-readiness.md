# Launch readiness checklist

## Product and manufacturer

- [ ] Written product specification: grade, size, moisture, seasoning load and sensory tolerance
- [ ] Approved contract manufacturer and backup supplier
- [ ] Food business licences verified for the actual activities and premises
- [ ] Bill of materials tied to one production batch
- [ ] Incoming QC and finished-goods release criteria
- [ ] Retained samples and complaint investigation process
- [ ] Shelf-life study protocol and approved storage conditions

## Pack and claims

- [ ] Ingredients in descending weight order
- [ ] Nutrition panel from the final formula and lab method
- [ ] Allergen declaration and cross-contamination review
- [ ] Net quantity, MRP, consumer care, batch, date and use-by fields
- [ ] FSSAI logo and licence details for brand owner and manufacturer as applicable
- [ ] Every nutrition, health and ingredient claim has written substantiation
- [ ] Ecommerce images show mandatory information before sale
- [ ] HSN and GST treatment confirmed for the finished product

## Supply and inventory

- [ ] Three comparable manufacturer quotes
- [ ] Purchase order lead time and minimum batch documented
- [ ] FEFO inventory tracking
- [ ] Lot traceability from supplier to customer/channel
- [ ] Weekly stock-age and projected-expiry report
- [ ] Recall contact tree and quarantine location

## Channel

- [ ] Signed deductions, payment cycle, returns, expiry and promotion terms entered in the model
- [ ] Node list and replenishment rule
- [ ] Product content, images and claims match the physical pack
- [ ] One named owner for in-stock rate and dispute reconciliation
- [ ] Full-price measurement window protected

## Go/no-go

The product does not ship if a critical food-safety, label or traceability item is open. A launch date is not a reason to waive a quality release.
