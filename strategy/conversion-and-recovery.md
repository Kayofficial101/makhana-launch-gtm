# Conversion journey and drop-off recovery

The journey starts at a real snack occasion, not at an impression.

| Stage | Buyer question | Brand action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger | I want something crunchy now. What should I get? | Show a familiar flavour, clear portion and immediate availability | Qualified reach by occasion and location |
| Evaluation | Will this taste good and is it worth the price? | Sampling, pack clarity, direct comparison and price test | Blind liking, price intent, alternative named |
| Paid trial | Is it worth spending on once? | ₹50 trial pack or controlled core-pack offer | Paid conversion, not free-sample interest |
| Core purchase | Is the larger pack better value for my occasion? | Make the ₹149 core pack the main shelf and app choice | Trial-to-core conversion and CM2 |
| Repeat | Do I want this again without a large discount? | Keep flavour and availability consistent; ask for reason if not | 4-week and 8-week repeat by discount cell |
| Bundle/referral | Is this useful to share, gift or recommend? | Mixed bundle and simple referral test | Bundle conversion and referred paid orders |

## Recovery table

| Drop-off | What it may mean | Recovery action | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tasted but did not buy | Taste, price or pack confusion | Ask one reason immediately; retest only the relevant variable | Paid conversion by reason |
| Viewed product but did not add | Weak main image, unclear pack or wrong price | Test one content change at a time | View-to-cart rate |
| Added but did not buy | Final price, delivery threshold or competing basket choice | One reminder where permitted; no repeated discounting | Cart-to-order conversion |
| Bought trial, not core | Core pack value is unclear or occasion is too small | Show per-gram value and a mixed core offer once | Trial-to-core rate |
| Bought once, no repeat | Taste, texture, portion or no recurring occasion | Short feedback message with reason codes | 8-week repeat and reason mix |
| Repeats only on offer | Price is unsupported at full value | Separate promotion cohorts; do not count this as healthy repeat | Full-price repeat |
| Product unavailable | Replenishment or forecast failure | Restore stock and suppress paid media in the affected node | In-stock rate and lost sessions |
| Complaint after purchase | Product or quality defect | Resolve, trace the lot and follow the quality process | Complaint rate and closure time |

## Measurement rules

- Free samples are not customers.
- Promo repeat and full-price repeat are separate cohorts.
- A reminder is tested once. Permanent incentives can hide a weak product.
- Marketing spend is paused in nodes that cannot stay in stock.
- Recovery is not used to rescue a product that fails the taste or quality gate.
