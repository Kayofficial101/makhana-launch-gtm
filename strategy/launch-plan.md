# Twelve-week launch plan

| Week | Work | Owner | Decision or output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruit interviews, buy competitor packs, align product brief | GTM lead | Approved research sample and benchmark set |
| 2 | Run 60 interviews, three price cells and manufacturer RFQ | Research + operations | Occasion, objections, WTP and comparable quotes |
| 3 | Formulation round one; package structure and claims review | Product + manufacturer | Two flavours move to blind test |
| 4 | 120 blind tests; formulation round two if required | Research + product | Taste gate and final pilot recipe |
| 5 | Nutrition, microbiology and shelf-life protocol; artwork proof | QA + regulatory | Release-to-print checklist |
| 6 | Pilot production and incoming QC | Operations + QA | Traceable saleable batch |
| 7 | Office/community tastings and 10-store start | Field lead | Conversion, complaint and stock-age baseline |
| 8 | Expand to 20-30 stores if quality and price hold | Sales | Store reorder and full-price velocity |
| 9 | Review commercial terms; prepare 40-60 q-commerce nodes | GTM + finance | Signed channel economics in model |
| 10 | Node launch with one controlled introductory offer | Channel lead | Exposed-node baseline |
| 11 | Cohort, CM2, in-stock and expiry review | Analytics + operations | Fix, hold or expand recommendation |
| 12 | Gate review and next-batch decision | Founder/GM | Stop, reformulate, continue or scale |

## Meeting rhythm

- Monday: stock, quality and supply review
- Wednesday: channel performance and field feedback
- Friday: experiment readout and decisions
- Every two weeks: formal gate review, with model updated using actual terms

## Evidence folder for a real pilot

The team should add dated files for manufacturer quotes, lab results, final labels, batch release, interview notes, taste data, platform terms and weekly cohorts. No commercial claim should survive in the deck if it cannot be traced to one of those records.
